# 24-second two-person recording

Status: shot plan only; live capture is blocked by unavailable browser access. No simulated footage has been substituted.

Prepare two separate signed-in browser profiles with non-sensitive demo callsigns (Em and Sam). Use a fresh room and a track you have permission to include in a public recording. Keep passwords, email addresses and invite keys out of the capture. Record at 1920 × 1080, 30 fps; crop to the application viewport. Capture system audio once from the host to avoid doubling it.

| 9–16s | Sam pastes a YouTube track URL into the queue and adds it. Show the same track appearing for Em. | Everyone brings a track. |

| 21–24s | Hold the room view with both names and the current track visible. | All Nighter · Lock in till sunrise. |

Before capture, verify both sessions are genuinely connected and playback syncs. Do not use an edited counter or a prerecorded player to imply a live connection. Export H.264 MP4 with AAC audio; keep a silent captioned version for autoplay feeds.
